Moon Pancakes 🌙

The easiest breakfast in the galaxy!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 0.4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

How to make them

  1. Preheat the griddle or frying pan.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  3. In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until just combined.
  5. Drop the batter onto the griddle or frying pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.

Chef's tip: For extra fluffy pancakes, let the batter rest for a few minutes before cooking.
